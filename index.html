Task List

Tasks and lists right in your browser

Task List is a Chrome extension for your to-dos: lists and sections, labels, due dates and reminders, a calendar, comments and attachments. It opens instantly from the toolbar or in the side panel, works offline and syncs between your devices — no sign-up needed.

  • Quick syntax: type “Call Anna tomorrow 15:00 #work p1”.
  • Save the current page as a task from the right-click menu or a shortcut.
  • Month and week calendar with drag-and-drop rescheduling.
  • Connect your other Chrome profiles with a one-time code.
